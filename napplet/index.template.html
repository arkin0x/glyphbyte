    Turn a link into glyphs

    Paste a link, or write a short note, and get six glyphs to draw. Anyone can scan the glyphs to retrieve your link or text.

    {{icon:circle-alert}}What you write is saved as a small public record that nobody can edit or delete afterwards, not even you. Check it before you make glyphs.
